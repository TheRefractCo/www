The reader is meant to get out of the way. Open a PDF or e-book from your library, then use the page as your starting point.

## Find your way around

Use the page controls to move forward and back, jump to a page, or adjust the way the document sits in the window. On a longer read, the library and document outline help you return without losing your place.

## A good first habit

Keep one small action nearby while you read: highlight a key sentence, write a question, or add a short note. These marks make it easier to come back later with context.

## When you are done

Close the document when you are ready. Your library remains the place to return to it, alongside the rest of your reading—not a queue you need to clear.
