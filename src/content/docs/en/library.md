The library is Paper’s front door. It gathers the documents you are reading and gives them enough structure to be found again without making you manage a database.

## Build a shelf that helps

Start with the documents you use. Add books, papers, and long reads as they become part of your current season of work or study.

## Return by recognition

Cover, title, and a familiar place on the shelf can be more useful than a deep folder tree. Keep the system light enough that you will actually keep using it.

Read [how to add a document](/paper/docs/getting-started/adding-library/) or [organize your shelf](/paper/docs/library/organizing/).
