Paper keeps reading and thinking close together. Use the reader for the document, then use ink and notes for the parts you want to keep.

## The page comes first

Reading controls should help you settle in: choose a comfortable view, move through the document, and keep the surrounding interface quiet.

## Add meaning as you go

Annotations are for the sentence you want to find again. Notes are for the thought that does not belong in the margin. Both can stay with the document until you are ready to share or revisit them.

Explore [annotating with Windows Ink](/paper/docs/reading/annotating/) or [keeping useful notes](/paper/docs/reading/notes/).
